Princes Street roof detail

Sources: Edinburgh, Scotland, two 4K drone clips by Nick Hawkes, published
21 January 2024 on Pexels. The frames were registered to the measured roofs,
rectified, masked for visibility, and combined into a 3D map texture.
The main deck retains its photographed colour. The adjacent roofs retain
local colour and surface detail from the footage as contrast over the map shading.
East view, 00:31: https://www.pexels.com/video/edinburgh-scotland-19951743/
North view, 00:29: https://www.pexels.com/video/edinburgh-scotland-19951740/

To regenerate from the original 4K clips:
ffmpeg -ss 31 -i <east.mp4> -frames:v 1 <east.png>
ffmpeg -ss 29 -i <north.mp4> -frames:v 1 <north.png>
python3 scripts/build-princes-street-roof-photo.py --detail-frame --north-frame <north.png> <east.png>
