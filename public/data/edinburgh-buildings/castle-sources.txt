The reference photographs are used to study the exterior. The asset contains
hand-authored geometry and procedural materials.

Castle Rock material
Rock colour, weathering and joints are generated procedurally. Surveyed slope
and surface heights guide shading and blending with the surrounding terrain.
The texture contains no satellite or reference-photo pixels.
Geology reference: British Geological Survey, The Geology of the Neighbourhood
of Edinburgh, Castle Rock basalt plug.
https://webapps.bgs.ac.uk/memoirs/docs/B01944.html
